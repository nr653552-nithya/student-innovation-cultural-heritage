# 🏛️ HeritageConnect

### Keep India's Heritage Alive 🇮🇳

HeritageConnect is a digital platform developed for **Smart India Hackathon 2026** under the theme **Heritage & Culture**.

The platform helps users discover India's rich cultural heritage, including **monuments, temples, festivals, food, dance, music, traditions, cities, and cultural stories** from different parts of India.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🔎 **Heritage Explorer** – Search for monuments, food, festivals, traditions, people, and cultural experiences.
- 🎤 **Voice Search** – Explore heritage information using voice commands.
- 📷 **Heritage Scanner** – Scan and identify heritage-related places or objects.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Map** – Discover cultural and heritage locations across India.
- 🌐 **Multilingual Support** – Supports multiple Indian languages.
- 📍 **Location Based Discovery** – Find heritage experiences based on location.
- 🔴 **Live Search** – Quickly search for cultural information.
- 🏛️ **Cultural Information** – Learn about India's history, traditions, art, architecture, and lifestyle.

## 💡 Our Solution

HeritageConnect provides a single digital platform where users can easily explore India's diverse cultural heritage.

Instead of searching different websites for information about monuments, festivals, food, traditions, and cultural places, users can discover everything through one simple and interactive platform.

The project aims to make Indian heritage **accessible, engaging, searchable, and easier to preserve digitally** for future generations.
